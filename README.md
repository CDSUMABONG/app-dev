# Favorite Anime

> *"No poison is purely harmful; in the right dosage, it becomes medicine."* — Maomao

## 1. The Apothecary Diaries (*Kusuriya no Hitorigoto*)

### Overview
Set in an alternate historical China, the story follows **Maomao**, a pragmatic young woman trained in herbal medicine. After being kidnapped and sold into service at the Imperial Palace's Rear Palace, her sharp mind and extensive knowledge of poisons quickly draw the attention of **Jinshi**, a high-ranking palace official.

### Key Details
| Attribute | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Genre** | Mystery / Historical / Drama / Slice of Life |
| **Protagonist** | Maomao |
| **Key Dynamic** | Maomao & Jinshi |
| **Studio** | OLM / TOHO Animation STUDIO |

### Favorite Highlights
* **Maomao's Poison Testing** — Her obsession with testing toxic substances on her own body for science.
* **Intricate Palace Intrigue** — Solving subtle mysteries behind consorts' illnesses and court politics.
* **Visual Aesthetic** — Lush animation paired with vibrant historical palace attire.

## 2. JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Steel Ball Run (*Part 7*)

### Overview
Set in an alternate 1890s America, **Steel Ball Run** chronicles a brutal, cross-country horse race spanning from San Diego to New York. Paralyzed former jockey **Johnny Joestar** joins the race after discovering that **Gyro Zeppeli's** mysterious *Spin* technique temporarily restores his ability to walk.

### Key Details
| Attribute | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Genre** | Action / Supernatural / Western / Adventure |
| **Main Duo** | Johnny Joestar & Gyro Zeppeli |
| **Primary Power Systems** | Stands & The Spin |
| **Author** | Hirohiko Araki |

### Favorite Highlights
1. **The Spin & Steel Balls** — A unique rotational energy technique deeply connected to the Golden Ratio.
2. **Johnny & Gyro's Brotherhood** — One of the best written partner dynamic in manga history.
3. **The Holy Corpse Quest** — High-stakes battles against opponents with bizarre Stand abilities.
4. **Funny Valentine** — A complex antagonist driven by extreme patriotism and the Stand *Dirty Deeds Done Dirt Cheap (D4C)*.
